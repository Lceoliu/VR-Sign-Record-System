# Separate capture completeness from capture quality

Quest continues to seal every recoverable Completed or Aborted Run, while reporting structural Capture Completeness separately from graded Capture Quality. Low tracking quality is retained as evidence and shown during Result Review instead of blocking or deleting the seal; only the headset wearer must confirm before returning to pre-start. This preserves diagnostic data and operator choice at the cost of carrying explicit quality policy and a terminal review state.
